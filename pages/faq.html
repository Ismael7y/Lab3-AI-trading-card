The card

What is this site?

A digital trading card made for a class lab, plus a small platform game, Cat World 1-1. You can build your own version of the card.

How do I change my card after setting it up?

Go to My Card and press Edit my card.

How do I reset my card to start over?

Press Edit my card, then Start over from the default card, then save.

Where is my card saved? Can other people see it?

Only in your own browser. It isn't sent anywhere, so other visitors see the default card, not yours.

Why does my card look different on another device or browser?

Each browser keeps its own copy, so a card made on your laptop won't show up on your phone.

Why can't a rating go above 99?

Like sports video game cards, ratings go from 0 to 99, and the bars on the card are sized to fit that range.

How do the card colors work?

You pick a main color (the header, buttons, and stat bars) and an accent color (the card's background tint and the picture box behind the cat). Choose a preset or press Custom to pick any color from a color wheel. Text colors adjust automatically, so the card stays easy to read whatever you pick.

The game

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The on-screen buttons show up under the game on touch screens. See Controls for what each one does.

The game won't respond to my keys.

Click the game first so it has focus. If it says PAUSED, press P or click it to keep going.

I jumped over the flagpole. Why didn't I finish?

You have to touch the pole itself. Jump into it instead of over it.

Accessibility and the site

Can I turn off the animations?

Yes. The site follows your device's "Reduce motion" setting, which stops the aura flames and the card tilt.

Can I use the site with only a keyboard?

Yes. Tab moves between links and buttons, Enter or Space presses them, and Escape closes the setup popup.
