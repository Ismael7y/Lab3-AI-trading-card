How to play Cat World 1-1

Help the black cat run through World 1-1 and touch the flagpole at the end before the timer runs out.

Keyboard controls

Click the game (or Tab to it) first so it can hear your keys.

Keyboard controls
ActionKeys
Move left / right← → or A / D
Jump (hold for higher)Space, ↑, W, or Z
RunShift or X
Start / restartEnter, or click the game
Pause / resumeP, or the Pause button

Touch controls

On a phone or tablet the on-screen buttons show up under the game. On a computer, turn them on with Show on-screen controls to play with a mouse.

◀ ▶
Run Jump
  • ◀ ▶ (left side): move left and right.
  • Run: hold it while moving to go faster.
  • Jump: hold it longer to jump higher.
  • You can hold a direction and tap Jump at the same time.
  • Tap any button to start a new game.

Rules

  • Jump on mice from above to stomp them. Touching one from the side costs a life.
  • You have 3 lives and 400 seconds.
  • Hit ? blocks from below for coins. Bricks break.
  • You can't walk back past the left edge of the screen.
  • The level only counts as cleared when you touch the flagpole. Jumping over it doesn't count.
  • The game pauses by itself if you switch tabs or click away.

Secret

Spoiler: a hidden bonus

If the last two digits of the timer match when you touch the flag (like 255 or 11), that many fish bursts go off, worth 500 points each.
